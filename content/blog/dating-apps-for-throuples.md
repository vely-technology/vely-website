Dating apps for throuples and couples should make the relationship structure clear rather than leaving a third person to discover important information later. A good profile communicates who is involved, what each person is looking for, and how conversations can begin safely.

## Features worth comparing

- Joint couple or group profile options
- Nearby discovery
- Mutual matching before messaging
- Blocking and reporting
- Privacy controls
- Clear adults-only community standards

## Safety checklist

Keep early conversations inside the app, do not send money, avoid sharing a home address, meet in a public place, and make sure every person has independent transport and a comfortable way to leave.
