A dating app for couples should make the couple visible from the start. When two people are dating together, clarity matters for everyone involved.

Start with a joint profile. Use photos that show both partners clearly and write a bio that explains what kind of connection you are open to. You do not need to share private details, but you should avoid vague language.

Discuss boundaries before matching. What kind of people are you open to meeting? Are you looking for friends, dates, other couples, or something more open-minded? What pace feels comfortable for both partners?

Use preferences honestly. If you only want to meet other couples, say that. If you are open to singles too, make that clear.

First conversations should be respectful and direct. Do not assume the other person or couple wants the same thing you do. Ask simple questions and give clear answers.

For first meetings, choose public places and low-pressure plans. Coffee, food, or an activity works better than a private setting.

Vely supports couples with joint profiles, nearby discovery, mutual matching, and in-app chat. That structure helps couples explore without hiding who they are or forcing awkward explanations later.
