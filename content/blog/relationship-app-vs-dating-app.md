People often use "relationship app" and "dating app" to mean the same thing, but the intent can be different.

A dating app usually helps people discover romantic or social matches. The focus is on profiles, likes, matches, and conversations.

A relationship app may suggest a more serious intent. People searching for relationship apps often want long-term compatibility, emotional connection, or a path toward commitment.

A social discovery app is broader. It can include dating, friendship, couples meeting couples, open-minded connections, and meeting new people nearby.

Vely sits in this social discovery space while still supporting dating and relationships. Singles can look for a partner, casual dating, friendship, or new connections. Couples can create a joint profile and discover other people or couples together.

The best app for you depends on your intent. If you want marriage-focused matching, a matrimony product may be more relevant. If you want to meet new people, date, or explore open-minded connections with control, a dating or social discovery app like Vely is a better fit.

Whatever you choose, look for clear profiles, safety controls, mutual matching, and easy reporting. Those features matter more than the label.
