The best dating app bios sound like a real person, not a resume. They give enough detail to start a conversation and enough clarity to attract compatible matches.

For singles, try a bio that mixes personality and intention:

"Coffee walks, live music, and quiet Sundays. New to Pune and looking to meet people who enjoy good conversation."

"Equal parts ambitious and low-key. I like bookstores, street food, and people who can communicate clearly."

"Here for something honest. Open to friendship, dating, and seeing where the connection goes."

For couples, clarity matters even more:

"Couple in Bangalore looking to meet other open-minded people for conversation, food plans, and genuine friendship."

"Together for four years, curious about meeting couples with similar energy. We value respect, consent, and easy conversation."

"Weekend cafes, travel plans, and board games. Looking for people who are kind, clear, and fun to talk to."

Avoid bios that are too vague: "just ask," "here for fun," or "I do not know what to write." These make it harder for someone to message you.

Also avoid long lists of rules. A short, clear profile usually performs better than a defensive one.

On Vely, profiles can represent singles and couples, so use the bio to make your intent clear from the beginning.
