Red flags get most of the attention, but green flags matter just as much. A green flag is a sign that someone may be emotionally safe, respectful, and capable of building a healthy connection.

Consistency is one of the biggest green flags. The person does what they say they will do. They reply when they can, show up on time, and do not disappear whenever the conversation gets real.

Respect is another clear signal. They respect your pace, your boundaries, your time, and your answer when you say no. They do not pressure you to meet sooner, share private details, or move the conversation off the app before you are comfortable.

Good communication is a green flag too. They can ask questions, answer honestly, and talk through small misunderstandings without turning everything into conflict.

Look for curiosity. A healthy match wants to know who you are, not only what you look like. They ask about your interests, values, daily life, and what kind of connection you want.

Emotional maturity also shows up early. They do not blame every past partner, make every conversation about themselves, or expect you to fix their loneliness.

Green flags do not guarantee a perfect relationship, but they help you choose better. On Vely, mutual matching gives you a calmer starting point. From there, pay attention to how the person behaves after the match.
