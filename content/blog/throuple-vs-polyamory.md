A throuple is a relationship involving three people. Polyamory is a broader approach to relationships that allows multiple romantic connections with the knowledge and consent of everyone involved. A throuple can be polyamorous, but not every polyamorous person is part of a throuple.

## The key difference

“Throuple” usually describes the number and structure of people in one relationship. “Polyamory” describes a relationship philosophy or practice that can include different structures, including triads, networks, or people who date independently.

Some throuples have a close three-person relationship. Others have different levels of connection between partners. There is no requirement that every relationship look identical.

## What they have in common

Both require consent, honesty, communication, and clear boundaries. Nobody should be pressured into a label, a pace, or an agreement that does not work for them.
