Nymph is newer and more tightly focused on ethical non-monogamy, with "role preference" and desire tags that make intent explicit from the first look. If you want reach, pick Feeld. If you want a smaller community where everyone has already stated what they are looking for, Nymph fits better. ## Who Feeld is for

Feeld has been around the longest and that shows in its user base. In most large cities it has the deepest pool of open-minded singles and couples, and it offers a wide menu of gender identities, sexualities and relationship structures. Couples can link profiles so that matches see both partners. The trade-off is noise: with scale comes more inactive profiles, more people unsure about what they want, and a paid tier that gates several of the filters you need to cut through that.

Feeld works best if you live in a big metro, want the most choice, and are willing to spend time filtering.

## Who Nymph is for

Nymph is built specifically for ethical non-monogamy. Its signature feature is **role preference**, where you state how you see yourself in a connection, plus desire tags that describe what you are open to. Because every profile carries that information, conversations start from a shared understanding instead of a guessing game. The community is smaller than Feeld's, so outside major cities you may run out of relevant profiles quickly.

Nymph works best if non-monogamy is the point, you want intent to be explicit, and you prefer a smaller, more deliberate community.

## What is role preference in Nymph?

Role preference is Nymph's way of letting you describe the part you want to play in a connection, for example as a single joining an existing couple, as one half of a couple, or as someone exploring on your own. Combined with desire tags, it tells other members what kind of connection you are open to before anyone sends a like. It is one of the clearest intent systems in the category, and the main reason people pick Nymph over Feeld.

## Feeld vs Nymph: five things that decide it

### 1. Community size versus clarity
Feeld wins on numbers. Nymph wins on knowing what each profile wants. Decide which problem annoys you more: too few options, or too many vague ones.

### 2. Couples support
Both support couples, but in different ways. Feeld links two individual accounts. Nymph treats the couple as an entity with shared preferences. If you want one joint profile that both partners manage, with partner verification, that is where Vely's [couple profile](/dating-app-for-couples/) model differs from both.

### 3. Non-monogamy as a feature versus the whole product
On Feeld, ENM is one of many structures. On Nymph, it is the product. If you are curious but not committed to non-monogamy, Feeld or a broader social discovery app is the gentler entry point.

### 4. Pricing
Both are free to start and charge for filters, incognito modes and seeing who liked you. Expect to pay on either if you want to search efficiently in a busy city.

### 5. Safety
Both offer verification, blocking and reporting. Neither can check who someone is in real life, so the usual rules apply: video call first, meet in public, and tell someone where you are going. Our [meeting safely as a couple](/blog/meeting-safely-couples/) guide covers the specifics.
