ENM, open relationship, and polyamory are related terms, but they are not always interchangeable. The simplest way to understand them is to treat **ethical non-monogamy (ENM)** as the broad category and open relationships and polyamory as relationship styles that may fit within it.

## Quick comparison

| Term | Usually means | Main focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ENM | Consensual relationships that are not strictly exclusive | Honesty, consent, and agreements |
| Open relationship | A partnership that allows some outside connections | The boundaries around outside dating or intimacy |
| Polyamory | The possibility of multiple romantic relationships | Love, connection, and more than one relationship |

These are common explanations, not fixed rules. People may use the labels differently, so ask rather than assume.

## What all three require

Healthy non-monogamous relationships require honest communication, informed consent, clear boundaries, and respect for privacy. Everyone should know enough to make a free decision, and agreements should be revisited as circumstances change.

## How to represent yourself on a dating app

Say whether you are single, partnered, or part of a couple. Explain what type of connection you are open to without promising something you cannot offer. If a partner is involved, make sure the profile and conversations reflect that person’s knowledge and agreement.

## The label is less important than the agreement

Two people can use the same label and want very different things. Ask about relationship structure, availability, boundaries, and expectations before assuming compatibility. Respect a no, and remember that consent can change at any time.
