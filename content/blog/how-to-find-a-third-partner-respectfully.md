When a couple wants to meet a third person, respect should come before the search. The third person is not a role to fill or an accessory to an existing relationship. They are a person with their own needs, boundaries, and right to change their mind.

## Be honest from the first profile

Say that you are a couple, identify who is participating, and explain what kind of connection you hope to explore. Do not use an individual profile to conceal that a partner is involved.

## Discuss expectations early

Talk about whether you want friendship, dating, a committed relationship, or something else. Discuss privacy, time, safer-sex practices, communication, and what happens if feelings develop differently.

## Avoid couple-first pressure

A third person should be able to build trust with each partner and make independent choices. Do not demand instant commitment, require identical feelings, or expect the new person to accept rules they did not help create.

## Meet safely

Keep early conversations inside the app, avoid sharing sensitive information, meet in public, and make sure everyone can leave comfortably. Vely supports couple profiles, nearby discovery, private likes, and mutual matching before chat.
