Couples in India have different reasons for joining a dating app. Some want to meet another couple, some want to make friends, and others are exploring a new kind of connection together. The best app should make those intentions clear from the beginning.

## What couples in India should look for

- A joint profile that represents both partners
- Nearby discovery in cities such as Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Pune, and Hyderabad
- Mutual matching before messaging
- Blocking and reporting tools
- Clear adults-only community rules
- Privacy controls that let both partners agree on what is shared

## Why mutual matching matters

A couple should not have to manage unwanted messages from strangers. Private likes and mutual matching create a simple boundary: conversation begins only when both sides express interest.
