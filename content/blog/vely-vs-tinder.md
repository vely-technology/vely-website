Vely and Tinder are designed around different starting points. Tinder is a large mainstream dating platform for individual users. Vely is focused on singles and couples who want a clearer social-discovery experience with mutual matching and local preferences.

## Vely vs Tinder at a glance

| Feature | Vely | Tinder |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Individual profiles | Yes | Yes |
| Couple profiles | Yes | Primarily individual profiles |
| Nearby discovery | Yes | Yes |
| Mutual matching | Yes | Yes |
| Best fit | Singles and couples wanting flexible social discovery | Singles using a mainstream dating network |
| Platform | Android via Google Play | Android and iOS |

## Choose Tinder if you want a large mainstream network

Tinder may be a better fit if your priority is a broad, familiar app centered on individual dating profiles and a large user base. As with any platform, your experience will depend on your location and the people active nearby.

## Choose Vely if you want singles-and-couples discovery

Vely may be a better fit if you want to join as an individual or couple, meet people nearby, and explain your connection goals more openly. Conversations begin after mutual interest, with blocking and reporting available in the app.
