Married couples looking for new connections need more than a large dating-app user base. The app should make it easy to show that both partners are involved, communicate boundaries, and decide together who can start a conversation.

## Questions to ask before choosing an app

### Can both partners be represented honestly?

A joint profile should explain who is participating. Use recent photos and write the profile together so nobody is surprised about the account or its intentions.

### Does the app support mutual matching?

Mutual interest helps prevent unwanted messages. It also creates a natural moment for both sides to confirm that they want to continue.

### Are privacy and safety tools easy to use?

Look for blocking, reporting, profile controls, and clear community rules. Avoid sharing home, workplace, financial, or identifying information too early.

## Set boundaries before matching

Agree on what you will share, whether both partners need to approve a match, what kinds of conversations are comfortable, and when you would stop. Consent should be ongoing for everyone involved.

For a first meeting, choose a public location, arrange independent transport, tell a trusted person where you are going, and leave if anyone ignores a boundary.
