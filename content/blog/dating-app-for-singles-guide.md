Getting better matches on a dating app is not only about looking good in photos. It is about giving people enough context to choose you for the right reasons.

Start with clear photos. Use a good face photo, one full-length photo, and one photo that shows something about your life. Avoid only selfies or only group photos.

Write a bio with specifics. Mention your city, a few interests, and what kind of connection you are open to. "Looking to meet thoughtful people for coffee, conversation, and maybe something serious" is more useful than "just ask."

Use preferences instead of swiping endlessly. If distance, age range, or connection type matters to you, set those filters honestly.

When you match, write a message that refers to the other person’s profile. A specific message almost always performs better than "hey."

Do not measure success only by match count. A small number of relevant matches is better than a large number of conversations that go nowhere.
