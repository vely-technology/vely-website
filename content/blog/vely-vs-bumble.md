Vely and Bumble serve different dating needs. Bumble is a mainstream app centered mainly on individual dating. Vely is designed for singles and couples who want local social discovery, clear profile intentions, and mutual matching before conversation.

## Main differences

| Question | Vely | Bumble |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Individual profiles | Yes | Yes |
| Couple profiles | Yes | Mainly individual profiles |
| Local discovery | Yes | Yes |
| Mutual interest before chat | Yes | Yes |
| Best for | Singles and couples with flexible connection goals | Individual users seeking mainstream dating |

Choose Bumble if you want a familiar mainstream dating experience for individual profiles. Choose Vely if you want a joint couple profile, to meet singles or couples, or to explore dating and friendship without narrowing your profile to one outcome.

Whichever app you use, be honest about your intentions, do not pressure anyone to move faster than they want, and keep your first meeting public. Vely users can also review the [Vely safety guidelines](/safety/) before meeting.
