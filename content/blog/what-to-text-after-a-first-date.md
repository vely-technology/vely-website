The text after a first date does not need to be complicated. It should be clear, kind, and honest enough to avoid confusion.

If you liked the date, send something specific: "I had a good time today. The cafe was a great choice, and I enjoyed talking about travel with you. Would you like to meet again next week?"

If you are interested but want to go slowly, say that: "I enjoyed meeting you. I would like to keep talking and see how it feels."

If you are unsure, do not fake certainty. You can write: "Thanks for meeting today. I had a nice time. I am still figuring out how I feel, but I am open to chatting more."

If you do not want to meet again, be respectful and direct: "Thank you for today. I do not think this is the right match for me, but I wish you well."

Avoid ghosting when the date was respectful. A short message is kinder and saves both people time.

Do not over-explain. You do not need to list every reason unless there was a serious issue.

If someone reacts badly to a polite no, that confirms your decision. Use block or report if needed.

On Vely, better matches start with mutual interest, but clear communication after meeting is still what builds trust.
