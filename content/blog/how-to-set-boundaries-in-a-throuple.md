Boundaries in a throuple are agreements about what each person is comfortable with and what they need to feel safe. They are not rules for controlling another person. A boundary should be clear, specific, and open to discussion as circumstances change.

## Areas to discuss

- How much time is shared and how much is individual
- Whether relationships are private or openly discussed with family and friends
- What information is shared about other dates
- Safer-sex and health agreements
- How decisions affecting all three people are made
- Money, travel, housing, and shared responsibilities
- How someone can pause or leave a situation

## Use a regular check-in

Choose a calm time rather than waiting for conflict. Ask what is working, what feels difficult, and whether any agreement needs to change. Each person should have room to speak without being outnumbered.

## Consent can change

Agreeing once does not mean agreeing forever. People can change their mind, ask for time, or end a connection. A healthy throuple makes room for that possibility without threats or punishment.
