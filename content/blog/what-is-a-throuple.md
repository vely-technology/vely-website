A throuple is a romantic relationship involving three people who understand and describe themselves as a relationship. Some throuples are committed long-term, some live together, and others keep separate homes. There is no single formula that every throuple follows.

## Throuple, triad, and polyamory

The word throuple is commonly used for a three-person romantic relationship. A triad is another term for a relationship involving three people. Polyamory is broader: it describes having or being open to multiple romantic relationships with everyone’s knowledge and consent.

Not every throuple is identical. Some have three connections between all partners, while others have a different structure. The people involved should explain their own relationship rather than relying on assumptions.

## What everyday life can involve

Throuples may share time, routines, travel, responsibilities, and emotional support. They also need individual time and space. Decisions about schedules, privacy, family, money, and future plans usually work best when everyone can speak openly.

## What makes a throuple healthy?

Trust, consent, honest communication, clear boundaries, and respect for each person’s independence are more important than the label. A throuple should not treat one person as an accessory to an existing couple or assume that one partner’s needs always come last.

## Common questions

### Is a throuple the same as a threesome?

No. A threesome usually describes an activity or encounter. A throuple describes a relationship structure, although people may use the terms differently.

### Can a throuple be committed?

Yes. Commitment depends on the agreements and actions of the people involved, not on the number of partners.

### Can a throuple live together?

Some do and some do not. Housing, finances, privacy, and practical responsibilities need to be discussed carefully.
