Before meeting someone from a dating app, run through a short safety checklist. It only takes a few minutes and can prevent avoidable risk.

First, check whether the profile feels consistent. Do the photos, bio, location, and conversation make sense together? If basic details keep changing, slow down.

Second, keep early conversations inside the app. If someone pushes you to move to another platform immediately, ask why. A respectful person will not pressure you.

Third, never send money. No emergency story from a new match should override this rule.

Fourth, choose a public first meeting place. Cafes, restaurants, malls, bookstores, and busy parks are better than homes, hotels, or isolated locations.

Fifth, arrange your own transport. Do not depend on a new match for pickup or drop-off.

Sixth, tell someone you trust. Share the person’s name, meeting location, and expected return time.

Seventh, keep personal information private. Do not share your home address, workplace details, financial information, Aadhaar number, passwords, or OTPs.

Eighth, trust discomfort. If something feels wrong before or during the meeting, leave or cancel.

Dating apps can help you meet good people, but safety is a combination of product features and personal habits. Vely supports mutual matching, reporting, and blocking so you can stay in control.
