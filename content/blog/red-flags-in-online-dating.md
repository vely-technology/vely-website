Online dating works best when you stay open but alert. Most people are normal, but a few warning signs should make you slow down or leave the conversation.

The clearest red flag is a money request. It may sound like an emergency, a travel issue, a medical problem, or a short-term loan. Do not send money to someone you have only met online.

Another red flag is pressure to move off the app immediately. Some people ask for WhatsApp, Instagram, or Telegram before trust exists. Keeping early conversations inside the dating app gives you access to blocking and reporting tools.

Be careful with inconsistent stories. If their job, location, age, or relationship status keeps changing, trust the pattern.

Overly intense affection too early can also be a warning sign. If someone talks about love, commitment, or future plans before you have had a real conversation, they may be trying to create emotional pressure.

Unsafe meeting plans are serious. A first date should be public, easy to reach, and easy to leave. Avoid private homes, hotels, isolated places, or late-night plans with someone you have never met.

If someone ignores your boundaries, that is enough reason to stop. You do not need to prove a person is dangerous before blocking them.

Vely includes mutual matching, blocking, and reporting, but your judgment still matters. Slow down when something feels wrong.
