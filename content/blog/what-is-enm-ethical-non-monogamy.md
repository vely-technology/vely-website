ENM means **ethical non-monogamy**. It describes relationships where everyone involved knows about the relationship structure and agrees to it. The word ethical is important: ENM is based on informed consent, honesty, and ongoing communication.

## Is ENM the same as cheating?

No. Cheating involves breaking an agreement or hiding a relationship from a partner. Ethical non-monogamy involves discussing the agreements openly and giving everyone the information they need to make their own choices.

ENM is not one single relationship model. It can include open relationships, polyamory, swinging, relationship anarchy, and other arrangements. Each person or couple defines their own boundaries.

## Common ENM questions

### Does ENM mean anyone can date anyone?

No. Every ENM relationship has boundaries. Partners may agree on who they date, how much information they share, safer-sex practices, time expectations, or whether romantic feelings are included.

### Is ENM only about sex?

No. Some people use ENM to describe romantic relationships with more than one person, while others focus on sexual openness or social connections. The meaning depends on the agreements people make.

### Can ENM relationships be serious?

Yes. ENM relationships can be casual, committed, romantic, sexual, or a combination. Relationship labels do not determine how much care or commitment people provide.

## How to approach ENM responsibly

Start with an honest conversation before using a dating app or meeting someone. Discuss what each person wants, what is not comfortable, how decisions will be made, and how someone can change their mind. Consent must remain ongoing.
